02 / MAKE IT DELICIOUS

Sushi rice, step by step

Good rice is the start of every great roll.

  1. Rinse the rice until the water runs clear.
  2. Bring the water to a boil.
  3. Add the rice, cover the pot, and reduce the heat.
  4. Cook on low until the water has been absorbed.
  5. Stir the vinegar, sugar, and salt until dissolved.
  6. Pour the seasoning over the rice and gently mix.
  7. Let the rice cool to room temperature before rolling.
